# Scoring rubric

Score each line 0–2 (0 = missing or wrong, 1 = partly, 2 = fully). Max 24.

| # | Criterion | 2 points when… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Simple result** | `PLAN.md` fits on about one screen, uses plain words, and the founder could follow it without opening anything else. |
| 2 | **Real blockers first** | Week 1 fixes the most important thing stopping the first client, found from the actual product (not generic advice). |
| 3 | **Fits the founder** | Every week's hours add up to the founder's hours; nothing they said they won't do; budget respected. |
| 4 | **Concrete places** | Every channel and action names a specific place, platform or person; no "use social media". |
| 5 | **Real people to contact** | 5+ targets that fit the ideal customer, are recent and reachable, with drafted first messages (or an honest "found N" plus search links). |
| 6 | **Honest goal check** | The goal is compared to a cited benchmark or labelled assumptions, with a floor and a target. |
| 7 | **No fabrication** | No invented numbers, quotes, rules, or founder experiences; unverified items are labelled. |
| 8 | **Focus** | 2 primary channels + 1 experiment (+ supporting/one-offs), with reasons for what was dropped. |
| 9 | **Plan B** | If/then rules and a day-30 milestone, including a second lever if the main bet fails. |
| 10 | **No filler** | No asset or section that the plan doesn't use; no repeated content across files. |
| 11 | **Consistency** | `PLAN.md`, `plan-data.json` and any exports say the same thing; `plan-data.json` passes `export.cjs --check`. |
| 12 | **Mode done right** | The tested mode's reference file was followed and produced what it promises (e.g. exports open, review updated the files). |

Also record: total tool calls, number of questions asked of the founder, and time to first useful output.
