# Emails mode: onboarding and retention sequences

Use when the founder wants welcome, onboarding, activation, retention or review-request emails (or in-app messages). Goal: a short sequence that gets new signups to the "aha" moment and brings quiet users back, written in the founder's voice, ready to paste into their email tool.

## 1. Know the path
From `product-profile.md` (or a quick look at the product): the signup step, the **activation event** (the first moment of real value, e.g. "first booking made", "first plan generated"), and what paying or staying looks like. Ask which email tool they use (or suggest a simple, cheap one for their stack) and whether they can trigger emails on events or only on time since signup.

## 2. The sequence (adapt, keep it short)
| # | When | Goal | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Right after signup | Get to value | Personal welcome from the founder, the ONE next step to the activation event, reply-to-me invitation |
| 2 | Day 1–2, if not activated | Remove friction | The most common blocker and how to get past it (from Phase 1's path to first value) |
| 3 | Day 3–4 | Show what's possible | One use case or example from a similar user (real, or clearly an example) |
| 4 | Day 7, if not activated | Ask why | Plain-text "what got in the way?" from the founder; replies are gold |
| 5 | After activation | Deepen | The next most valuable feature or habit |
| 6 | Trial end / renewal (revenue) | Convert | What they've done so far, the price, the launch offer if any, an easy way to ask questions |
| 7 | 2–3 weeks after value | Proof and referral | Ask for a review or testimonial (with explicit consent) or a referral |
| 8 | 30 days inactive | Win back | What's new, one reason to come back, an easy unsubscribe |

Physical products: order confirmation, shipping, delivery + how to use, review request after use, and a reorder/referral offer.

## 3. Writing rules
- From the founder, plain text or very light formatting, 50–150 words, one link or one ask per email.
- Subject lines: specific and honest, no clickbait; give 2 options each.
- Use the customer language from `feedback.md` or research when available. Only claim what's true.
- Legal: send marketing emails only to people who signed up or agreed (EU: consent for marketing; transactional emails are different), always include an unsubscribe link and a postal or legal address where required. Flag; don't give legal advice.

## 4. Measure
For each email: open rate is unreliable (privacy features distort it); track clicks, replies and, most of all, the activation rate of people who got the sequence vs. before.

## 5. Save
Write `marketing/assets/emails.md` (sequence table, then each email: trigger, subject options, body). Add "set up the sequence" (with hours) to `PLAN.md` and `plan-data.json`. Offer an Excel export of the sequence table if it helps them set it up in their tool.
